## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Specific — strongly. This is not category-interchangeable UI. The drum-machine-panel grammar is authored all the way down: segmented meters that peg and tell the truth in numerals, machined bevels/screws as the only depth cue in 2D, candy zones doubling as module identity, a chase pulse that is the *only* sanctioned glow, failure performed in the same hardware language (band blink like a dying battery, steel shards, meters counting down). The plug anatomy (tapered chrome tip → insulator groove → shaft → color band → knurled grip → strain relief) is a brand commitment actually delivered — in closeup it reads as the real object. Missed-specificity notes: the *opening* composition (a lone anchor jack floating mid-air) undersells the world; the right-hand fog murk swallows cube zones; module blues 05/06 overlap.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI found 2 findings on `index.html`: (1) `design-system-color` advisory — `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` (the strip's machined top bevel, line 44) not present as a token in DESIGN.md's frontmatter palette — a bookkeeping gap, since the value *is* documented in DESIGN.md prose; (2) `dark-glow` warning — zero-offset `box-shadow` glow `#f2d43a` on `.hud-seg.lit`. The in-page detector found 4: `undersized-ui-text` (9px "CABLE PATCH SANDBOX", `.hud-name-sub`), `dark-glow` (same lit segment), `wide-tracking` (0.20em on `.hud-hint`), `flat-type-hierarchy` (9/10/11/13/15/16px, ratio 1.8:1). **False positives (by intent, judged against DESIGN.md):**
- `dark-glow` on lit segments — the One Glow Rule *sanctions exactly this*: the LED bloom of a lit segment is live state, inseparable from its meaning. Removing it would break the meter grammar. Intentional; keep. (It is, however, the precise boundary a slop detector cannot see — worth a comment token in DESIGN.md so future runs can ignore-list it.)
- `wide-tracking` on the hint + `flat-type-hierarchy` — both are the silkscreen genre itself (DESIGN.md pins 9–15px, 0.14–0.34em, line-height 1). Genre-intentional; the *legibility* consequence is nonetheless real (see P1 #1) — the hint is functional text wearing costume size.

## Overall Impression

A coherent, honest, deeply authored instrument that already performs its proof: cords feel physical, links reward, failure is watchable. The single biggest opportunity is **first-contact legibility** — the door into the toy (the hint) and the meaning of its dying states are both whispers in a world that otherwise speaks clearly. Everything else is tuning.

## What's Working

1. **The link pulse is the product.** In captures the amber chase segment reads as a compact traveling light mid-sag — instantly legible as "signal flowing," ramping in/out at the jacks. The memorable moment the brief pinned is delivered (town-hall measure #4).
2. **The panel's honesty contract holds under vision inspection.** Zero lit pixel without state; meters peg past 12 and the numeral tells the truth; the hint vanishes the moment a cord exists; DOM flips are instant (no transitions). This is rare discipline.
3. **Plug anatomy and seat reads.** Closeups resolve tip/groove/shaft/band/grip/boot; seated plugs read perpendicular and planted; red/blue polarity is unmistakable at closeup range.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The first-run invitation fails its one job.** "PRESS N FOR A NEW CORD" is 10px/600 at 0.2em tracking, Dim Ink 6.70:1, centered in a 1440×900 dark stage — vision review called it "easily missed… needs to be larger/brighter." A first-time visitor's entire onboarding rests on this line (there is no other affordance until it's discovered by accident). Cross-validated: detector's `undersized-ui-text`/`wide-tracking` land on the same strip.
   *Fix:* raise the hint within the grammar — 12px, Legend Ink (#c3c8d1) or the amber state ink, weight 700; optionally a one-time sim-clock fade-up. Keep the honesty rule (gone once a cord exists).
   *Suggested command:* `$impeccable clarify`
2. **[P1] The failure grammar is illegible at the moment it matters.** The popped cord's ~3s grace is a subtle dim; the blink arrives only in the final second; the shatter shards read as "a few pixels" at default camera distance; and when a cord vanishes, nothing ever says why. Town-hall measure #3 demands the failure path read as *deliberate* — it plays, but it doesn't speak. (The aria summary counts states but never explains the death.)
   *Fix:* make dying loud in the panel's own grammar — earlier/brighter band blink, a shard scale/form bump (still zero glow), and have the live-region sentence name the event once ("1 cord popped — re-plug before it goes").
   *Suggested command:* `$impeccable clarify` (semantics) + `$impeccable animate` (sim-clock cadence only — no CSS tweens)
3. **[P2] State inks drift in situ.** In the rendered frame the LINKED row's jade reads amber/gold-warm next to the CORDS amber (vision called the distinction "compromised"), and the blue output end reads teal/gray at carry distance — against the brand commitment that polarity "reads instantly at full-frame distance." Numerals and labels carry redundancy, but the color channel itself is blurred.
   *Fix:* widen the amber/jade perceptual gap (cooler/brighter jade keyline, or warmer amber) and boost cobalt saturation/band width at distance.
   *Suggested command:* `$impeccable colorize`
4. **[P2] The opening composition reads "broken."** The anchor cord's jack hangs from an invisible pin at (0,1.6,0); vision review read it as "floating with no visible attachment… looks broken/placeholder." This is the first thing anyone sees (M1 heritage staging).
   *Fix:* give the anchor a visible mount in the panel language (a machined stud/bracket), or restage the opening as a plug seated on a cube with its partner resting on the bench.
   *Suggested command:* `$impeccable polish`
5. **[P2] Abandoned dropped cords never self-clean.** PRODUCT.md promises "self-clean when abandoned," but only *popped* cords clean; a dropped cord (both ends free) lies forever, and the only removal paths are the undiscoverable seat-then-release-off-cube trick or RESET (which clears everything). Tidiness friction for the creator mid-evaluation.
   *Fix:* a long-idle grace for never-seated dropped cords (the popped-cord machinery, extended), staying inside the approved lifecycle.
   *Suggested command:* `$impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: The only instruction is the near-invisible hint (P1 #1). The floating anchor reads as a bug (P2 #4). When their first cord dies they get no explanation anywhere on screen (P1 #2). Red/blue polarity meaning is never stated — cosmetic by spec, but Jordan doesn't know that and may hunt for a rule.
**Alex (Power User)**: N/R accelerators + modifier guards are right. No Esc-to-cancel a carry; no way to discard an unwanted cord short of RESET (P2 #5); picking priority means grabbing "the cube" where a plug is seated actually pulls the plug — correct by spec, surprising once.
**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: Keyboard floor (N/R + summary + labeled buttons + amber focus bracket at 11.95:1) is real and verified; plugging needs the mouse — disclosed in the canvas label per the approved floor. Residual: 9–10px functional text is hostile to low vision even at AA contrast (the hint again), and state changes are announced as counts, not causes.
